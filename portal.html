1. Master Schedule of Rates (HSR/CSR/CPWD) Explorer

Search tens of thousands of departmental items and insert them into estimates with 1 click.

Estimating
info Purpose & Overview

The Schedule Explorer gives you instant, searchable access to the complete official schedules of Haryana PWD (B&R), Public Health & Water Supply, Irrigation, and CPWD. Eliminates manual rate book browsing, typing errors, and outdated rate lookups.

open_in_browser Where to Access

In Google Sheets™, click menu CivilMate → Open Sidebar. The Schedule Browser is located on the primary Search Items panel.

format_list_numbered Step-by-Step Instructions
  • 1
    Select Department Source: Use the Source dropdown to select your target schedule (e.g. Haryana PWD B&R 2021, Public Health Engineering, or CPWD DSR).
  • 2
    Filter by Chapter: Optionally filter by Chapter (e.g. Chapter 3: Earthwork, Chapter 4: Plain & Reinforced Concrete, Chapter 10: Structural Steel) to narrow results.
  • 3
    Search Keywords: Type key terms into the search bar (e.g., M25 concrete, TMT Fe 500, excavation ordinary rock). The search highlights matched terms in real time.
  • 4
    Quick Insert vs. Cart Batch Mode:
    • Click the Bolt Icon (âš¡) to instantly drop the item into your currently selected row on the estimate sheet.
    • Or click the Checkbox to add multiple items to your cart, then click Insert Selected Items at the bottom to inject them sequentially.
lightbulb
Pro Tip for Non-Schedule (NS) Items: If an item is not found in the standard schedule, click Create NS Item in the sidebar. Enter a custom item description, unit, and analyzed market rate. CivilMate automatically issues a standardized NS code and saves it to your permanent catalog for all future estimates.

2. Automated Measurement Book (MB) & Dimension Math

Structure parent work items with child measurement rows (Nos × L × B × D) and in-cell math.

Estimating
info Purpose & Overview

Civil engineering estimates require strict separation between the parent bill item (item code, schedule description, rate, total quantity, total amount) and the detailed Measurement Book lines (location, Nos, Length, Breadth, Depth/Height). CivilMate automates this parent-child hierarchy with live formula injection.

format_list_numbered Step-by-Step Instructions
  • 1
    Insert Parent Item: Place an item from the Schedule Explorer onto your active sheet. The item appears in a parent row with Item Code, Schedule Description, Unit, and Rate.
  • 2
    Add Measurement Child Rows: Select the parent row or any child row below it. In the sidebar, set the number of child rows (e.g. 3) and click Add Measurement Rows.
  • 3
    Enter Dimensions (Nos, L, B, D):
    • Column H (Nos): Number of identical structural units (e.g., 2).
    • Column I (Length): Dimension in meters (e.g., 4.50).
    • Column J (Breadth): Dimension in meters (e.g., 3.00).
    • Column K (Depth/Height): Dimension in meters (e.g., 0.15).
  • 4
    Live In-Cell Math: You do not need a separate calculator. Type direct arithmetic expressions into dimension cells (e.g., 2*3.2+0.5 or 12.5/2). CivilMate automatically calculates the numeric value while preserving your expression in memory.
  • 5
    Execute "Magic Compile": Click Magic Compile in the sidebar. CivilMate scans the canvas, links parent total quantity cells to sum up all respective child rows, and evaluates parent row amounts (Total Qty × Rate) automatically.
calculate
Standard Measurement Formula: Child Qty = Nos × Length × Breadth × Depth
Parent Total Quantity = Σ(Child Quantities); Parent Total Amount = Parent Total Qty × Approved Rate

3. Handling Deductions, Openings & Voids

Apply automated bracketed minus deductions for doors, windows, and structural voids.

Estimating
info Purpose & Overview

Under IS 1200 measurement standards for brickwork, RCC, plastering, and painting, deductions must be recorded for door openings, window frames, lintel embeds, and pipe penetrations. CivilMate detects deduction keywords and formats them with standard bracketed minus calculations.

format_list_numbered Step-by-Step Instructions
  • 1
    Add a Measurement Row under the Item: Under your main item (e.g., Brickwork in Superstructure), add a measurement line for the deduction.
  • 2
    Include the Keyword "Deduct": In the description column, type Deduct Door D1 Opening or Deduct Window W1 Void.
  • 3
    Enter Dimensions as Positive Values: Enter Nos = 2, L = 1.20, B = 0.23, H = 2.10.
  • 4
    Automatic Negative Formulation: CivilMate automatically recognizes the Deduct keyword, renders the text in distinctive bold/italic deduction format, and computes the quantity with a negative multiplier (-1 × Nos × L × B × H).
verified
IS 1200 Plastering Rule Compliance: For plastering, openings under 0.5 m² require no deduction. For openings between 0.5 m² and 3.0 m², deduction is made for one face only. CivilMateâ€™s deduction engine allows manual multiplier overrides (e.g. Nos = -0.5) to reflect single-face plastering deductions precisely.

4. Cross-Item Quantity Linking & Multi-Unit Converter

Link quantities between related items dynamically and convert between Metric and FPS units.

Estimating
info Purpose & Overview

Civil quantities are fundamentally interlinked: the concrete volume of a column determines the formwork surface area, and earthwork excavation directly informs backfilling volume. Quantity Linking creates dynamic cell references between items, eliminating double-entry and updating all dependent rows whenever dimensions change.

format_list_numbered Step-by-Step Instructions
  • 1
    Open Quantity Linker: Select the child measurement cell or parent quantity cell you want to populate. In the sidebar, click Link Quantity.
  • 2
    Select Source Item: The Linker panel lists all items currently on your active canvas. Select the source item (e.g. Excavation for Foundation).
  • 3
    Choose Link Mode: Choose Direct Total Qty, Specific Measurement Row, or apply a Multiplier Factor (e.g. 0.10 for 10% foundation bed concrete). Click Apply Link.
  • 4
    Using the Unit Converter: Select any cell or range with dimensions. In the sidebar, click Unit Converter. Convert between Feet/Inches → Meters, Sq.Ft → Sq.M, or Cu.Ft → Cu.M with 1 click.

5. Steel Bar Bending Schedule (BBS) Calculator

Calculate cutting lengths, bend deductions, rebar unit weights, and batch into measurement rows.

Tools Suite
info Purpose & Overview

Bar Bending Schedules (BBS) require exact cutting lengths, bend deduction adjustments, and rebar tonnage summaries. CivilMateâ€™s modeless BBS tool allows you to schedule beams, columns, footings, and slabs in a floating dialog and batch-insert them straight into your estimate canvas.

open_in_browser Where to Access

Click menu CivilMate → CivilMate Tools (or click Open Engineering Tools in the sidebar). Select the Steel BBS tab on the left navigation bar.

format_list_numbered Step-by-Step Instructions
  • 1
    Enter Member Context: Specify the structural component name (e.g., Beam B1 (Span 4.5m) or Column C1 Ground Floor).
  • 2
    Select Bar Role & Diameter: Select the functional role (Main Bars, Stirrups / Ties, Distribution Bars, or Extra Top/Bottom). Select the bar diameter: 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 25, or 32 mm.
  • 3
    Input Cut Length & Bar Count:
    • Cut Length: Enter straight length in mm (e.g. 4850).
    • No. of Members: Number of identical beams/columns (e.g. 4).
    • Bars per Member: Number of bars per component (e.g. 3).
  • 4
    Live Weight Preview & Queue: CivilMate automatically calculates the unit weight using Weight = (d² / 162) × Length and displays the total weight in kilograms. Press Enter or click Add Entry to queue the bar into the summary table.
  • 5
    Inject to Active Sheet: Once all bars for the structural member are queued, click Inject to Active Sheet. CivilMate transfers the itemized rebar measurements directly into your active measurement sheet.
calculate
Standard Rebar Formulas (IS 2502 & IS 456):
• Unit Weight: w = d² / 162.2 kg per meter run (where d is diameter in mm).
• 90° Bend Deduction: -2d per bend.
• 45° Crank Deduction: -1d per bend; 135° Hook Deduction: -3d.
• Standard Hook Allowance: 9d for each 180° hook or 10d for 135° seismic stirrup hook.

6. Structural Steel Sections Handbook (IS_SP_6)

Model hot-rolled beams, channels, angles, and custom plates with automatic weight calculation.

Tools Suite
info Purpose & Overview

Structural steel estimation requires looking up section weights in the SP:6 (Part 1) Handbook. CivilMate embeds the official handbook directly inside the tool. Selecting any standard section or custom plate automatically populates its weight per meter run and aggregates total tonnage for technical sanction.

format_list_numbered Step-by-Step Instructions
  • 1
    Open Structural Steel Tool: In CivilMate Tools dialog, switch to the Structural Steel tab.
  • 2
    Choose Standard Profile vs. Custom Plate:
    • Standard Section Mode: Select category (e.g. Beams (ISMB), Channels (ISMC), Equal Angles (ISA), Unequal Angles, Joists).
    • Custom Plate Mode: Enter custom plate thickness (mm), width (m), and length (m). Density is calibrated to standard structural steel density (7,850 kg/m³).
  • 3
    Select Profile Designation: From the dropdown, choose your profile (e.g., ISMB 250 [37.3 kg/m] or ISA 75x75x6 [6.8 kg/m]).
  • 4
    Enter Length & Quantity: Input the length in meters (e.g. 6.50) and total number of pieces (e.g. 4). The live preview badge instantly displays the computed weight in kg and metric tonnes (MT). Click Add Section.
tips_and_updates
Gusset Plate Allowance Rule: Standard departmental specifications allow a 5% to 7% wastage/rivet/weld allowance on overall structural steel tonnage. You can add a dedicated child line item for Splice & Gusset Plate Allowance (5%) linked directly to the main structural steel total.

7. Earthwork & Trench Excavation Modeler (IS 1200)

Calculate excavation pits, sloped trenches, auto IS 1200 working offsets, and backfill balancing.

Tools Suite
info Purpose & Overview

IS 1200 (Part 1) mandates that for foundations deeper than 1.0 meter, an additional working allowance of 0.60m shall be added to each vertical face. Manually calculating these offsets and balancing positive excavation volume against negative backfill deductions is a frequent source of error. CivilMate automates both rectangular and sloped trapezoidal excavation profiles.

format_list_numbered Step-by-Step Instructions
  • 1
    Open Earthwork Tool: In CivilMate Tools, click the Earthwork tab.
  • 2
    Select Operation: Choose Excavation (+) for earth digging or Backfilling / Deduction (-) for filling trenches around masonry.
  • 3
    Select Profile Mode:
    • Rectangular (Auto IS 1200 Offsets): Check the box IS 1200 Working Allowance (+0.6m each side). Enter your net foundation dimensions (e.g. L = 2.0m, B = 2.0m, D = 1.5m). CivilMate automatically calculates the excavation volume using (2.0 + 1.2) × (2.0 + 1.2) × 1.5 = 15.36 m³.
    • Trapezoidal / Sloped: For deep trenches or loose soil, specify bottom width, depth, and side slope ratio (e.g. 1:1 or 1:1.5). The tool applies the mean sectional area method: Area = (b + s×d) × d.
  • 4
    Transfer to Sheet: Click Add to Measurement Queue. Queue both the excavation line and the backfill line to get the net surplus disposal volume automatically.
calculate
IS 1200 Mean Sectional Area Formula: Section Area = (B + s × D) × D
Where B = bottom bed width, s = horizontal slope ratio (s:1), D = trench depth. Total Volume = Section Area × Length.

8. Column Footings & Foundation Modeler

Calculate isolated, stepped, and sloped trapezoidal footings with prismoidal concrete math.

Tools Suite
info Purpose & Overview

Sloped trapezoidal footings are standard in reinforced concrete construction to save concrete while resisting punching shear. However, calculating the sloped volume manually using the prismoidal formula is slow and error-prone. This tool calculates the PCC bed, rectangular base, trapezoidal slope, and shuttering contact area simultaneously.

format_list_numbered Step-by-Step Instructions
  • 1
    Open Footing Tool: In CivilMate Tools, click the Footings tab. A clear visual schematic displays all required dimension labels.
  • 2
    Enter Base Dimensions: Enter bottom rectangular footing dimensions: Length (L), Breadth (B), and lower vertical depth (D1, e.g. 0.25m).
  • 3
    Enter Pedestal Top Dimensions: Enter the top pedestal column dimensions: top length (l), top breadth (b), and vertical slope height (D2, e.g. 0.35m).
  • 4
    Instant Multi-Item Breakdown: CivilMate computes three distinct items:
    • PCC Base M10: Base dimensions plus standard 100mm projection.
    • RCC Concrete M25: Lower rectangular volume + prismoidal sloped volume.
    • Shuttering Surface: Vertical perimeter formwork area: 2 × (L + B) × D1.
  • 5
    Transfer: Click Add Footing Schedule to send the entire grouped foundation breakdown to your active measurement sheet.
calculate
Prismoidal Formula for Sloped Trapezoidal Footing:
V_slope = (D2 / 3) × [ (L × B) + (l × b) + √( (L × B) × (l × b) ) ]
Total Concrete Volume = (L × B × D1) + V_slope.

    • Pipe Barrel Running Meters (R.M.)
    • Lean Concrete Cradle Bedding M15 (m³)

11. Contractor Running Bills Management (B1, B2 … Final)

Generate sequential running account bills with persistent cumulative memory and audit trails.

Billing
info Purpose & Overview

Contractor billing in public works operates on running account bills (1st Running Bill B1, 2nd Running Bill B2, up to the Final Bill). Each bill must reference the original tender DNIT quantity, track previously paid quantities, calculate current bill execution, and apply approved contractor premium or rebate.

format_list_numbered Step-by-Step Instructions
  • 1
    Generate Next Bill Sheet: In the Master Sidebar, click Create Next Bill (B#). CivilMate inspects your workbook sheets, identifies the next sequence number (e.g., B1, B2), copies the standardized Bill template, and updates the Project Switchboard index.
  • 2
    Populate Billed Quantities: In Column L (Current Qty), enter the executed quantities measured in the field for this billing cycle.
  • 3
    Automated Rate Evaluation: Column U evaluates the payable amount: if an approved tender rate or contractor premium is specified in Column T, it computes Current Qty × Approved Rate; otherwise it uses the standard schedule amount.
  • 4
    Execute Bill Compile: Click Magic Compile. CivilMate updates all billing totals, verifies cumulative math, and prepares the bill summary footer.

12. Automated Tender DNIT Variations & Deviation Hook

Automatically flag excess quantities and trigger statutory deviation approval warnings.

Billing
info Purpose & Overview

Public works contract clauses dictate that if executed quantities exceed the original Notice Inviting Tender (DNIT) provision, a formal Deviation Statement and Technical Sanction approval is mandatory before releasing payment. CivilMate monitors every bill item and flags deviations automatically.

format_list_numbered Step-by-Step Instructions
  • 1
    Automatic Formula Injection: On any bill sheet starting with "B", CivilMateâ€™s billing engine automatically injects formulas into Column X and Column Y for all parent items.
  • 2
    Variation Calculation (Col X): Evaluates = DNIT_Qty (Col W) - Previously_Billed (Col V) - Current_Qty (Col L).
    • A positive result means remaining available quantity within tender limits.
    • A negative result indicates excess quantity executed in the field.
  • 3
    Automatic Compliance Warning (Col Y): If Column X is less than zero, Column Y immediately triggers the alert: "Variation required", alerting technical officers that a revised sanction is required under contract rules.
warning_amber
Departmental Audit Compliance: When preparing the final bill, filter Column Y for "Variation required". Every flagged item must have an accompanying Deviation Statement before submission to the Divisional Accounts Officer (DAO).

info Purpose & Overview
