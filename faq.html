General & Setup
CivilMate is a powerful, cloud-based Google Sheets™ Add-on built exclusively for civil engineers, contractors, and government officials. It transforms a standard spreadsheet into a highly automated estimating and billing ecosystem, allowing you to manage construction data, perform complex structural calculations, and generate engineering reports instantly.
No. Because CivilMate operates entirely within Google Workspace™, there are no large files to download or complex installations. It takes less than 10 seconds and just 3 clicks to install. You can access your estimates anywhere—on a PC, Mac, tablet, or even via mobile setups like Samsung DeX and Pixel Desktop.
Absolutely. CivilMate is an independent, third-party tool that operates securely within your own Google account. Your measurement books, estimates, and billing data remain safely stored in your personal Google Drive™. We do not collect, view, or sell your project data.
